The 75 JSON outline assets in src/client/resources/assets/minecraft/darkwinter/highlights/
are unmodified outline definitions from Highlight. Only the resource folder was renamed
from resourcefullib/highlights to darkwinter/highlights. The complete original MIT license
is retained beside this notice and in the distributable JAR under META-INF/licenses.

DarkWinter uses its own reload parser, bounded model-edge extraction and native 26.3
feature-renderer integration. ResourcefulLib and Highlight JARs are not bundled.
